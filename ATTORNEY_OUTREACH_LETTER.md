**THE CASE IN BRIEF:**

My wife Arianna and I were both employed at Mercy Health in Springfield, Missouri. We were terminated on June 9, 2025. Mercy's stated reason was "Falsification of Time." We believe this is pretext for unlawful retaliation and discrimination. The following events occurred in the weeks leading up to our termination:

- **May 14, 2025** — I filed a workers' compensation claim for an ankle injury sustained on the job
- **May 19–20, 2025** — We reported sexual harassment
- **May 30, 2025** — The alleged "timecard incident" occurred
- **June 9, 2025** — Both of us were terminated simultaneously

**WHY THE "TIME FRAUD" CHARGE IS FALSE:**

My job title was linen truck driver. My duties required me to leave the main building regularly to deliver to outbuildings and transport linens and trash across campus. Leaving the building without clocking out was not fraud — it was the required function of my position. I performed my job the same way every single shift, exactly as I was trained to do from day one.

My wife Arianna's physical timeclock badge was non-functional for an extended period. She submitted all time entries from her PC at her desk, each of which required supervisor approval from Jordan Dubois before becoming final. Arianna could not have "falsified" any entry that her supervisor approved. Jordan Dubois was subsequently also terminated by Mercy.

**ADDITIONAL CLAIMS:**

- **ADA / Disability Discrimination:** I have a recorded phone call with a Mercy representative named Vicki in which I disclosed my disability to her three separate times and stated that I have difficulty reading. She continued without any acknowledgment or accommodation each time. This recording is in the case file.
- **Workers' Compensation Retaliation:** I was terminated approximately three weeks after filing a workers' comp claim.
- **Sexual Harassment Retaliation:** Both plaintiffs were terminated weeks after reporting sexual harassment.
- **Arianna's Additional Damages:** As a direct result of the termination and immediate loss of health insurance, Arianna was forced to cancel a scheduled surgery. She had been completing the required pre-operative appointments and test results, but could not finish that process without insurance. The surgery cancellation is a concrete, documented harm caused by the wrongful termination.
