## YOUR DEFENSE ON THE "TIME FRAUD" CHARGE

### Barry's Situation

Your job title was **linen truck driver**. Your duties physically required you to:
- Leave the main building to deliver to outbuildings
- Drive across campus picking up trash and linens
- Be off the main campus as a normal part of the work shift

Leaving the building without clocking out was not fraud — **it was the job**. You were trained to do it that way from your first day. You followed the same practice every single shift. The security video Mercy produced (May 30, 2025, 11:13 PM, Heart Hospital West Interior Entrance) shows you doing your job, not committing fraud.

**Key point to make clear at deposition:** *"That is how I was trained. That was the actual job. I did it the same way every single shift."*

### Arianna's Situation

Arianna's physical ID badge/timeclock card was **non-functional** for an extended period. Because of that, she submitted all her time entries **from her PC at her desk** — the only option available to her. Every single submission went to her supervisor **Jordan Dubois** for approval before it became final in the system.

This means one of two things is true — and either one destroys Mercy's case:
- **If Dubois approved the May 30 entry** → he finalized it, not Arianna. She cannot have "falsified" something her supervisor signed off on.
- **If Dubois did not approve it** → the system flagged it automatically, nothing was finalized, and there is no falsification.

**Additional fact:** Jordan Dubois was **also subsequently terminated by Mercy.** The reasons are unknown, but his termination is worth exploring in discovery.
